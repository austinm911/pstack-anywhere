## Changes and decisions

Two new skills: `sharpen` and `create-formal-verification-skill`. Both are
harness-neutral and ship unchanged. The pack now contains 55 skills, 54 of them
gated by `disable-model-invocation`.

Upstream cut the autopilot-full, autopilot-stack, and shipping playbooks down to
principles and moved PR landing to a bottom-up, one-at-a-time flow without `gt`.
The rewrites are accepted whole. Their Cursor primitives are mapped as before:
owners and verifiers spawn through `spawn_worker`, the hourly audit and landing
watch use `wake_on_event` with an explicit no-scheduler report, live lanes use
the `ui_driver` or `cli_driver` role and name a missing driver, the slop-strip
uses the `slop_strip` role or `unslop`, review bots resolve through
`review_automation`, and ticks re-read playbooks from the installed skill root.
The owner decision trail goes to the program's `.pstack/runs/<run-id>/` store
in place of the Cursor agent store.

The new "Match the model to the job" rule in poteto-mode's Subagents section is
kept with model slugs, `-fast` suffixes, and the Task tool replaced by role
lines, the harness's speed and reasoning controls, and `spawn_worker`. The
appended "pick each subagent's reasoning level" sentences in playbooks and
routed skills are kept verbatim. Swarm drops its "cloud workers" sentence as
upstream did. Orchestrate takes the "chain rebases" wording.

The orch frontier now walks the PR base chain through `gh` or `origin` rather
than `gt`. The three orchestration files stay byte-identical and the manifest
was regenerated from the selected source. The worktree audit merges upstream's
Origin-or-gh PR lookup with this port's transcript-coverage handling.

Also fixed a prior-port regression in `opening-a-pr.md`, where "Branch from
trunk" had been rewritten to "Branch from the installed skill root".

Upstream added a native Claude Code plugin install from a generated tree
(`docs/harness/claude-code.md`, `harness/pstack/`). It is omitted here because
the port already installs skills directly into every harness.
